KNIFE CLIENT 2.1 - FLAT VERSION. Every file goes in the repo root, no folders.

HOW TO UPDATE (GitHub website):
1. Open your knifeclient repo -> "Add file" -> "Upload files".
2. Drag in EVERYTHING from this folder (Ctrl+A). Say yes to replace files.
3. Your .github/workflows/main.yml stays as is.
4. OPTIONAL clean-up: you can delete the old tex_karambit.png, tex_bayonet.png, tex_butterfly.png,
   tex_huntsman.png, tex_flip.png, tex_falchion.png (they are not used any more; leaving them is harmless).
5. Wait for the green tick under Actions, download the artifact, put the jar in mods.

IN GAME:
 Right Shift = menu (pages on the left, just type to search, right-click a card for its settings)
 V           = inspect your knife (change in Controls)
 Hold C      = zoom
 Menu -> Knives page = pick from 16 knives, colour, animation options.
